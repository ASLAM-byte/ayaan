Summer Matcha Nights Are Coming Near You

Late-open evenings, cold pours, and a soundtrack for the golden-hour crowd. Here's how we're turning the warmest months into a slow, green ritual.

Summer changes the way a city drinks. The espresso rush softens, mornings stretch a little later, and the appetite shifts toward something cold, green, and unhurried. This season we're leaning all the way in.

A different kind of evening

Starting in July, we're keeping the doors open past sundown on weekends. Think chilled ceremonial pours, a rotating cast of fruit-forward specials, and playlists curated by the same baristas who somehow always know what you needed to hear.

Matcha was never only a morning drink. It's a tempo — and summer is the season to slow it down.

We'll be pouring iced versions of the whole signature menu, plus a few limited-run collaborations we've been quietly testing behind the bar. The strawberry matcha, unsurprisingly, is already the crowd favorite.

What to expect

Expect gatherings that feel less like events and more like the good part of a house party — low lighting, easy conversation, and no pressure to be anywhere else. Bring a friend, bring a book, or bring nobody at all. The seat is yours either way.

Dates and the full summer calendar drop soon on our journal and socials. Until then, the afternoons are long and the matcha is cold. We'll see you here.
