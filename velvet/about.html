The Atelier

A small room, good light, and a rule we don't break: make less, make it well.

We started Velvet Promise to make the kind of pieces we couldn't find — quiet, honest, and built to be kept.

Hand-cut, in small runs.

Each style begins as a single toile, refined until it drapes the way we want. Only then do we cut — in runs small enough that every piece is checked by the person who made it.

We keep the range narrow on purpose. Fewer things, made with attention, tend to outlast whatever was trending the week they were bought.

Materials we can stand behind.

Pure mulberry silk and French leavers lace, sourced from mills we've visited. Natural fibres, low-waste cutting, and offcuts kept for repairs.

Every order can come home for mending, for as long as you own it. That's the promise in the name.
