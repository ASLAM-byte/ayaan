A Story

Every wedding is really a stack of small moments — the ones that never make it onto an invitation but somehow decide everything. This is one of ours, written down before we forget the details we swear we never will.

How it actually went

It was less tidy than the version we tell at dinner parties and far better than either of us expected. There was a plan; the plan quietly fell apart; what remained was the part that mattered.

The best parts were never the ones we planned.

If you're reading this as a guest, thank you — genuinely. The whole point of gathering everyone at the barn is that these small moments get a few more witnesses. We can't wait to make some new ones with you there.
